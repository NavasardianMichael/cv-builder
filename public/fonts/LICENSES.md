# CV fonts — provenance and licences

Forty Armenian fonts from [fonter.am's popular list](https://fonter.am/en/fonts?sortby=popular).
Every one is labelled `free` on its own fonter.am page, whose licence line reads **"The font is
free for commercial or personal use."** Each was downloaded from that page; the author is the
page's "Author / Company".

They are the first forty `free` fonts in popularity order: the first twenty chosen 2026-10-29
(rechecked 2026-11-02: same ranking, licences and authors), the next twenty added 2026-11-02.
**Left out** of the top 53: SHK Dzeragir (#4), Eurotype 2016 (#34) and FMBF Wien (#50), which
are non-commercial; Arial (#7, proprietary Microsoft font, not needed); and ArmAllegro (#8),
ArTarumian Barak (#22), ArmCoronet (#23), Armenian Decorative (#26), ArTarumian Anpuit (#31),
Sos Banff (#32), Sylfaen (#40), ArTarumian Yerevan (#49), Arshaluyse Art (#52), which state no
licence.

Two downloads hold more than the font: Kamavor-NA's zip also carries an unrelated Latin-only
font, `Maler.ttf`, which is not shipped; "Nortar & Nortar Body" is two families, of which the
text face, Nortar Body, is shipped.

Glyph gaps, identical in fonter.am's original files (nothing was lost in conversion): Reactive
has Armenian capitals only, no lowercase; Artsakh and Kamavor-NA lack և « »; NASA Font, Amar,
BERD, Noto Sans Armenian and Noyemi lack « » (Amar also ։); CosiArm lacks ՝ « »; Kotayk lacks
։ ՝ « »; Arm Hmk's Bebas Neue and Braind Gyumri lack ՞; Braind Republic lacks ։. The browser
draws a missing glyph in the next face of the stack (`cvFontStack`: Mardoto).

## How the files were made

`scripts/fonts/build_fonts.py` converts each downloaded zip to woff2, subset to Latin,
Latin-1/Extended-A, Cyrillic, Armenian and punctuation, and writes `manifest.json`;
`pnpm fonts:css` turns the manifest into `cv-fonts.css`. Subsetting is a format conversion
the licences above permit; nothing was otherwise modified. Neither OFL font names a Reserved
Font Name, so subsetting them may keep the name.

Embedding note: the font `90` declares bitmap-only embedding (OS/2 fsType 0x200), so Chrome
may draw it as outlines in a PDF rather than embedding it — it still looks the same, but text
in that font may not be selectable.
